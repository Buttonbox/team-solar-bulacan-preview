A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you go solar.

Will solar keep my power on during an outage?

A standard grid-tied system typically shuts down during an outage. Backup power requires a compatible hybrid system and a correctly sized battery and backup circuit.

How much can I save?

Savings depend on your electricity use, tariff, solar production, system design, and how much energy you use during daylight. The team should assess these before providing an estimate.

Where can I see the actual projects?

The system details are drawn from Team Solar Bulacan’s public project posts. Open the Facebook links in the project cards for the original installation photos. The photos on this demo website are generated illustrative visuals.
